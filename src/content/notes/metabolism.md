title: Metabolism

Every day, we ingest nutrients, which primarly come in the form of proteins, sugars and fats (_macronutrients_).
Our body needs proteins, sugars and fats to function properly.
For one, these are the building blocks out of which the body builds itself.
But apart from that, they can also be burned provide the energy needed to function (and do all the building).

To further complicate things, the exact macronutrients we ingest are not necessarily the same that the body needs in that moment.
The _metabolism_ is the process by which the body solves this.
It consists of two parts: _catabolism_[^a] and _anabolism_.[^b]
The catabolism breaks ingested macronutrients up into smaller pieces and energy (primarily ATP), while the anabolism uses energy to synthesise these small pieces into the desired building blocks.

At the heart of this lies the _citric acid cycle_ (CAC), also known as _Krebs cycle_, after Hans Krebs.
